Homophones for kids

Homophones sound the same but have different spellings and meanings. Learning them in pairs, with what each one means, helps you pick the right word when you write.

Practise in the app

Year 4 homophones (20 pairs)

  • their / there their = belonging to them; there = in that place
  • there / they're there = in that place; they're = they are
  • to / too to = towards; too = also / excessively
  • your / you're your = belonging to you; you're = you are
  • its / it's its = belonging to it; it's = it is / it has
  • whose / who's whose = belonging to whom; who's = who is / who has
  • here / hear here = in this place; hear = to listen with your ears
  • write / right write = to put words on paper; right = correct, or a direction
  • knew / new knew = past tense of know; new = not old
  • know / no know = to understand something; no = not any / opposite of yes
  • sea / see sea = a large body of salt water; see = to look with your eyes
  • sun / son sun = the star at the centre of our solar system; son = a male child
  • flower / flour flower = the bloom of a plant; flour = a powder used for baking
  • hour / our hour = a unit of time (60 minutes); our = belonging to us
  • meat / meet meat = food from an animal; meet = to come together with someone
  • piece / peace piece = a part of something; peace = calm, without war
  • break / brake break = to smash or split; brake = used to stop a vehicle
  • weather / whether weather = the conditions outside, like rain or sun; whether = used to show a choice between options
  • ate / eight ate = past tense of eat; eight = the number 8
  • blue / blew blue = a colour; blew = past tense of blow

Year 5 homophones (8 pairs)

  • accept / except accept = to agree to receive or take something; except = not including; other than
  • affect / effect affect = to influence or change something (verb); effect = the result of a change (noun)
  • principal / principle principal = the head of a school; principle = a basic rule or belief
  • stationary / stationery stationary = not moving; stationery = paper and pens used for writing
  • complement / compliment complement = something that completes or goes well with something else; compliment = a nice comment about someone
  • desert / dessert desert = a dry, sandy area of land; dessert = a sweet food eaten after a meal
  • allowed / aloud allowed = given permission; aloud = out loud, so it can be heard
  • cereal / serial cereal = a breakfast food made from grain; serial = a story told in parts, one after another

Year 6 homophones (8 pairs)

  • council / counsel council = a group of people who make decisions for a town or area; counsel = advice given to someone
  • currant / current currant = a small dried grape; current = happening now, or a flow of water or electricity
  • stalk / stork stalk = the stem of a plant; stork = a large wading bird with a long beak
  • bridal / bridle bridal = relating to a bride or wedding; bridle = a set of straps used to control a horse
  • canvas / canvass canvas = a strong, heavy cloth used for painting or tents; canvass = to ask people for their opinions or votes
  • draft / draught draft = an early version of a piece of writing; draught = a current of cool air in a room
  • mantel / mantle mantel = a shelf above a fireplace; mantle = a cloak, or a role or responsibility someone takes on
  • waive / wave waive = to choose not to use a right you have; wave = to move your hand as a greeting, or a moving ridge of water
