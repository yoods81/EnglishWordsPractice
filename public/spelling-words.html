Spelling words for Years 4, 5 and 6

Tricky words are easier to spell when you have a memory hook. Every word below comes with a tip. Look at the word, say it out loud, cover it up and try to write it, then practise by typing in the app.

Practise in the app

Year 4 spelling words (49 words)

  • believe Remember: i before e, except after c.
  • because Think: Big Elephants Can Always Understand Small Elephants.
  • beautiful 'Beauty' + 'ful' — remember the 'eau'.
  • different Two syllables can hide: dif-fer-ent.
  • favourite Australian spelling keeps the 'u' — favour + ite.
  • separate There's 'a rat' in sep-a-rate.
  • definitely There's 'finite' hidden inside definitely.
  • necessary One collar, two sleeves: 1 'c', 2 's's.
  • surprise Don't forget the silent 'r' — sur-prise.
  • language Lang + u + age, not 'languege'.
  • February Don't forget the first 'r' — Feb-ru-ary.
  • Wednesday Wed-nes-day — the 'd' is silent.
  • although 'Al' + 'though' — say each part slowly.
  • thorough Th-orough, not 'thor-ow'.
  • government Don't forget the 'n' before 'ment' — govern + ment.
  • environment Don't forget the 'n' — environ + ment.
  • immediately Double 'm' — im + mediately.
  • occasion Double 'c', single 's' — oc-ca-sion.
  • recommend One 'c', double 'm' — re-com-mend.
  • disappear Double 's', single 'p' — dis-ap-pear.
  • disappoint Double 's', double 'p' — dis-ap-point.
  • embarrass Double 'r', double 's' — em-bar-rass.
  • excellent Double 'l' — ex-cel-lent.
  • opposite Double 'p' — op-po-site.
  • possible Double 's' — pos-si-ble.
  • address Double 'd', double 's' — ad-dress.
  • achieve i before e, except after c — a-chieve.
  • receive i before e, except after c — re-ceive.
  • friend 'Fri' + 'end' — a friend to the end.
  • knowledge Silent 'k' — know + ledge.
  • island Silent 's' — i-s-land.
  • answer Silent 'w' — an-s-wer.
  • listen Silent 't' — lis-ten.
  • straight Str + aigh + t — think of a straight line.
  • though T-h-o-u-g-h — not pronounced how it looks.
  • through Th-r-ough, like 'threw' but spelt differently.
  • guard Silent 'u' — g-u-a-r-d.
  • guide Silent 'u' — g-u-i-d-e.
  • vegetable Don't forget the middle 'e' — veg-e-table.
  • interesting In-ter-est-ing — five syllables, don't skip any.
  • library Two 'r's hiding — lib-r-a-r-y.
  • probably Prob-ab-ly — don't drop the middle 'a'.
  • actually Act + u + ally, not 'actully'.
  • especially E-special-ly — 'special' is hidden inside.
  • experience Ex-peri-ence — say each part slowly.
  • restaurant Rest + au + rant — tricky French spelling.
  • neighbour Silent 'gh' — neigh + bour.
  • height H-e-i-g-h-t, rhymes with 'kite'.
  • weight W-e-i-g-h-t, rhymes with 'gate'.

Year 5 spelling words (20 words)

  • accommodate Double 'c', double 'm' — ac-com-mo-date.
  • appearance 'Appear' + 'ance', not '-ence'.
  • argument No 'e' after argu- — ar-gu-ment.
  • calendar Ends in '-ar', not '-er'.
  • cemetery Three e's — cem-e-tery.
  • colleague Co + lleague, with a silent ending.
  • committee Double 'm', double 't', double 'e'.
  • conscience Con + science — listen to your conscience.
  • curiosity Curi(ous) + osity.
  • desperate Des-per-ate, not 'desprate'.
  • exaggerate Double 'g' — ex-agg-er-ate.
  • familiar Famil + iar, not '-ier'.
  • foreign Silent 'g' — this word breaks the 'i before e' rule.
  • fascinating The 'sc' makes an 's' sound — fas-ci-nating.
  • grateful Grate + ful, not 'greatful'.
  • humorous 'Humour' drops the 'u' before '-ous'.
  • jewellery Jewel + l + ery (Australian spelling).
  • leisure E before i — an exception to the usual rule.
  • maintenance Main + ten + ance.
  • mischievous Mis-chiev-ous, not 'mis-CHEEV-ee-ous'.

Year 6 spelling words (20 words)

  • accidentally Accident + ally — double 'l' at the end.
  • acquire Silent 'c' after 'a' — ac-quire.
  • bureaucracy Bur-eau-cra-cy — tricky French-style spelling.
  • conscientious Con-science + tious.
  • correspondence Cor + respond + ence.
  • millennium Double 'l', double 'n' — mill-enn-ium.
  • entrepreneur French origin — en-tre-pre-neur.
  • exhilarate Ex + hil + a + rate.
  • incidentally Incident + ally.
  • inoculate One 'n', one 'c' — in-oc-u-late.
  • liaison Lia + i + son.
  • manoeuvre Australian spelling: man-oeu-vre.
  • occurrence Double 'c', double 'r' — oc-cur-rence.
  • parliament Par + lia + ment — the 'ia' is easy to miss.
  • perseverance Per + severe + ance.
  • privilege No 'd' — priv-i-lege.
  • questionnaire Double 'n', French ending '-aire'.
  • rhythm No standard vowels — only a 'y'.
  • silhouette Sil + hou + ette.
  • unnecessary Double 'n', double 's'.
